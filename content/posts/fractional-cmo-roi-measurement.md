## The Right KPIs to Measure Success

Don't measure what's easy. Measure what matters to the business.

### Tier 1 KPIs: Revenue-Aligned Metrics

These directly impact business outcomes.

**1. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**

**Formula:** Total marketing + sales cost ÷ New customers acquired

**Benchmark (B2B SaaS):**
- Early stage: $5K-$15K
- Growth stage: $10K-$30K
- Enterprise: $30K-$100K+

**What to track:**
- CAC trend (decreasing = good)
- CAC by channel
- Payback period (goal: <12 months)

**Fractional CMO impact:** 20-30% CAC reduction typical

---

**2. Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**

**Formula:** Average revenue per customer × Average customer lifespan × Gross margin

**Benchmark (B2B SaaS):**
- Should be 3-5X your CAC
- Annual churn: 5-7% acceptable
- Net revenue retention: 100-120% good, 120%+ excellent

**What to track:**
- LTV trend (increasing = good)
- Churn rate
- Expansion revenue

**Fractional CMO impact:** Improved ICP targeting increases LTV by attracting better-fit customers

---

**3. CAC:LTV Ratio**

**Formula:** LTV ÷ CAC

**Benchmark:**
- **3:1 to 5:1** = Healthy and sustainable
- Below 3:1 = Inefficient, slow growth
- Above 5:1 = Underinvesting in growth

[Industry research shows](https://gripped.io/b2b-saas/b2b-saas-cac-ltv-metrics-guide/) that the ideal LTV ratio remains 3:1—meaning for every $1 spent acquiring a customer, you should generate at least $3 in lifetime revenue. The actual median for SaaS companies in 2025 hovers between 2.5:1 and 3:1.

**What to track:**
- Ratio trend over time
- Ratio by acquisition channel
- Impact of ICP refinement on ratio

**Fractional CMO impact:** Strategic focus on ideal customers improves both sides of ratio

---

**4. Revenue Attribution**

**What to measure:**
- Marketing-sourced revenue (deals originated by marketing)
- Marketing-influenced revenue (marketing touchpoints in buyer journey)
- Revenue by channel
- Revenue by campaign

**Benchmark:**
- Marketing-sourced: 30-50% of total revenue
- Marketing-influenced: 70-90% of total revenue

**Fractional CMO impact:** Better attribution modeling reveals true marketing contribution (often higher than companies think)

---

**5. Pipeline Velocity**

**Formula:** (Number of opportunities × Average deal value × Win rate) ÷ Sales cycle length

**What this measures:**
- How fast revenue is moving through pipeline
- Marketing's impact on sales cycle acceleration

**Benchmark:**
- 10-20% improvement in first year is strong performance

**Fractional CMO impact:** Better lead quality and sales-marketing alignment shortens sales cycle

---

### Tier 2 KPIs: Leading Indicators

These predict future business outcomes.

**6. Marketing Qualified Leads (MQLs)**

**Benchmark:**
- 35% increase in first year (documented case studies)

**What to track:**
- Volume trend
- MQL-to-opportunity conversion rate (15-25% typical)
- Quality score (sales feedback)

**Red flag:** MQL volume up but conversion rate down = quality problem

---

**7. Conversion Rates by Funnel Stage**

**Benchmarks (B2B SaaS):**
- Visitor to lead: 1-5%
- Lead to MQL: 10-20%
- MQL to opportunity: 15-25%
- Opportunity to customer: 20-30%

**What to track:**
- Stage-by-stage conversion
- Bottlenecks in funnel
- Improvement trends

**Fractional CMO impact:** Strategic focus on weakest conversion points drives overall performance

---

**8. Deal Velocity & Sales Cycle Length**

**Benchmark (B2B SaaS):**
- SMB: 30-60 days
- Mid-market: 60-120 days
- Enterprise: 120-180+ days

**What to track:**
- Average days in each stage
- Trend (decreasing = good)
- Marketing's impact on acceleration

**Fractional CMO impact:** Better lead quality and content for buyer journey reduces cycle time

---

### Tier 3 KPIs: Brand & Market Position

Harder to measure but important for long-term value.

**9. Brand Search Volume**

**What to measure:**
- Branded search volume trend
- Share of voice in market
- Brand awareness (surveys)

**Why it matters:** Indicates market awareness and demand generation working

---

**10. Market Share of Voice**

**What to measure:**
- Your mentions vs. competitors
- Analyst recognition
- Industry presence

**Why it matters:** Leading indicator of market position and future demand

## What NOT to Measure

These metrics feel productive but don't predict revenue.

### Vanity Metrics to Ignore

❌ **Social media followers**
- Correlation with revenue: Near zero
- What matters instead: Engagement from ICP, leads generated

❌ **Website traffic (total)**
- Correlation with revenue: Low
- What matters instead: Traffic from ICP, conversion rate

❌ **Email open rates**
- Correlation with revenue: Low
- What matters instead: Reply rate, meeting booked rate

❌ **Impressions or reach**
- Correlation with revenue: Near zero
- What matters instead: Engagement from target accounts

❌ **Content published (volume)**
- Correlation with revenue: None
- What matters instead: Content that drives pipeline

> **Measurement Reality:** [Research emphasizes](https://www.geisheker.com/how-can-i-measure-the-success-of-a-fractional-cmo/) that in B2B, especially in longer sales cycles, the real lift comes from the quality of pipeline, strategic clarity, and systems that compound—not vanity metrics.

## ROI Calculation Framework

### Method 1: Direct Revenue Impact

**Formula:**
```
ROI = (Revenue attributed to CMO initiatives - CMO cost) ÷ CMO cost × 100
```

**Example:**

Fractional CMO cost: $150K/year

Revenue impact:
- Improved conversion (30 extra deals × $25K ACV): $750K
- Channel optimization savings: $50K
- Faster sales cycle (opportunity cost): $100K

Total impact: $900K

ROI = ($900K - $150K) ÷ $150K × 100 = **500% ROI**

---

### Method 2: Cost Avoidance Comparison

**Formula:**
```
ROI = (Cost of full-time CMO - Fractional CMO cost + Value created) ÷ Fractional CMO cost × 100
```

**Example:**

Full-time CMO total cost: $500K/year
Fractional CMO cost: $150K/year
Value created (CAC reduction, efficiency): $200K

ROI = ($500K - $150K + $200K) ÷ $150K × 100 = **367% ROI**

---

### Method 3: CAC Payback Improvement

**Formula:**
```
ROI = (Old CAC payback - New CAC payback) × Monthly new customers × 12 ÷ CMO cost × 100
```

**Example:**

Old CAC payback: 18 months
New CAC payback: 12 months (6-month improvement)
Monthly new customers: 10
Average deal: $30K ARR

Value of 6-month acceleration: $30K × 10 customers × 6 months = $1.8M faster revenue realization

Annual value (conservative): $300K

ROI = $300K ÷ $150K × 100 = **200% ROI**
