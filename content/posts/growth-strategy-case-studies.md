## Growth Strategy Framework: Building Scalable Marketing Systems

As a pre-revenue company, we're transparent about our current stage while sharing the strategic frameworks that will help growth-stage companies scale effectively.

The 8-week strategy sprint methodology is designed specifically for companies between $1-5M in revenue facing common growth challenges.

## The Strategic Framework We Use

Our 8-week strategy sprint is built around a proven framework that addresses the core challenges growth-stage companies face:

**1. Strategic Foundation (Week 1-2)**
- Revenue model analysis and unit economics
- Competitive positioning and market mapping
- Ideal customer profile definition with disqualifiers
- Customer journey mapping and conversion bottlenecks

**2. Tactical Framework (Week 3-4)**
- Channel prioritization based on customer acquisition cost
- Budget allocation framework with ROI targets
- Campaign templates and messaging guidelines
- Attribution model setup and measurement infrastructure

**3. Operational Systems (Week 5-6)**
- Custom dashboard connecting all marketing systems
- Lead scoring and qualification processes
- Campaign optimization playbooks
- Weekly reporting and decision-making frameworks

**4. Team Enablement (Week 7-8)**
- Process documentation for independent execution
- Team training on strategic decision-making
- Handoff procedures for agencies or contractors
- 30-day post-engagement support

## Common Challenges We Address

**Data Fragmentation**
Most $1-5M companies have marketing data scattered across 4-7 different systems. We build unified visibility so you can see what actually drives revenue.

**Unclear Positioning**
Many companies try to serve everyone, which means they serve no one well. We help clarify who you serve best and why they should choose you.

**Tactical Without Strategic**
Strong execution but unclear priorities leads to scattered efforts. We provide the strategic framework to focus marketing investments.

**Attribution Blindness**
Companies run campaigns but can't measure impact. We build measurement systems that show marketing's contribution to revenue.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to see results from a strategy sprint?

Strategy sprints deliver strategic architecture in 8 weeks. Initial improvements in strategic clarity and team alignment happen immediately. Measurable business impact typically shows within 90-120 days as the strategic framework takes effect and teams execute independently.

### What's the difference between strategy sprint and traditional marketing consulting?

Strategy sprints deliver complete strategic architecture in 8 weeks with full ownership transfer and training included. Traditional consulting provides ongoing advisory over 6-12 months. The sprint model focuses on building your capability; traditional consulting focuses on providing guidance and oversight.

### Can strategy sprints work for companies outside $1-5M revenue range?

The 8-week sprint model is designed specifically for companies between $1-5M where strategic foundation is needed but full-time CMO economics don't make sense. For companies outside this range, we can discuss whether the framework can be adapted to their specific needs and constraints.

### What happens if the strategy doesn't produce expected results?

The sprint delivers complete strategic architecture regardless of business outcomes. You own the positioning, frameworks, dashboards, and playbooks. The infrastructure enables you to diagnose issues and adjust independently without ongoing consulting dependency. The focus is on building capability, not guaranteeing specific business results.
