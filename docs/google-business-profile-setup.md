# Google Business Profile Setup Guide

## 🎯 **Why This Matters**
- **Local SEO**: 46% of Google searches have local intent
- **Trust signals**: Verified business listings rank higher
- **Rich snippets**: Show star ratings, hours, and contact info in search results
- **Local pack**: Appear in the top 3 results for local searches

## 📋 **Step-by-Step Setup Process**

### **Phase 1: Claim Your Listing (Week 1)**

#### **1. Search for Existing Listing**
- Go to [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com)
- Sign in with your business Gmail account
- Search for "Pattern Growth" or your business name
- If found: Click "Claim this business" and verify ownership
- If not found: Click "Create a business with this name"

#### **2. Choose Primary Category**
**Recommended primary category:** `Marketing Consultant`
**Additional categories:**
- Business Consultant
- Business Management Consultant
- Marketing Agency
- Internet Marketing Service

#### **3. Complete Basic Information**
```
Business Name: Pattern Growth
Address: [Use your business address or service area]
Phone: [Your business phone number]
Website: https://www.patterngrowth.com
Description: "We deliver CMO-level marketing strategy through focused 8-week sprints. Growth-stage companies get complete strategic architecture—positioning, measurement systems, and playbooks—without ongoing consulting dependency."
```

#### **4. Verify Your Business**
- **Mail verification** (recommended): Google sends postcard to address (5-14 days)
- **Phone verification**: Instant but less preferred for SEO
- **Email verification**: If you have business domain email

---

### **Phase 2: Optimize Your Profile (Week 2)**

#### **1. Complete All Sections**
- ✅ **Business hours**: Set to your typical availability
- ✅ **Phone number**: Use a local number if possible
- ✅ **Website**: Link to your homepage
- ✅ **Services**: List your core offerings
- ✅ **Products**: If applicable
- ✅ **Attributes**: Check all that apply (free consultation, etc.)

#### **2. Service Areas**
Since you're a consultant, set up service areas instead of a physical address:
```
Primary Service Area: San Francisco, CA
Additional Areas: Bay Area, Silicon Valley, Remote Services
```

#### **3. Add High-Quality Photos**
- **Logo**: Use your current logo (square format, high resolution)
- **Cover photo**: Team photo or office space
- **Interior photos**: If you have an office
- **Team photos**: Professional headshots
- **At work**: Show your process or tools

#### **4. Write SEO-Optimized Description**
```
"Pattern Growth delivers CMO-level marketing strategy through focused 8-week sprints. We help growth-stage companies ($1-5M revenue) build complete marketing infrastructure—strategic positioning, measurement systems, and executable playbooks—without ongoing consulting dependency.

Our project-based approach means you own everything we create: dashboards, frameworks, and processes your team can run independently. No monthly retainers, no long-term commitments, just strategic clarity delivered fast.

Services include:
• Growth strategy development
• Marketing infrastructure setup
• KPI dashboard creation
• Team training and handoff
• Fractional CMO alternatives"
```

---

### **Phase 3: Content & Reviews (Ongoing)**

#### **1. Add Services with Descriptions**
```
Growth Strategy Sprint
"8-week project delivering complete marketing strategy architecture. Includes positioning, measurement systems, campaign playbooks, and team training."

Fractional CMO Alternative
"Project-based strategic consulting without ongoing retainers. Get CMO-level thinking delivered as a fixed-scope engagement."

Marketing Strategy Consulting
"Build marketing infrastructure your team can actually run. Strategic frameworks, dashboards, and processes designed for independence."
```

#### **2. Request Reviews**
**Timing**: Ask after successful project completion
**Process**:
1. Complete a successful project
2. Send follow-up email: "How was your experience working with us?"
3. If positive response: "Would you mind leaving us a review on Google?"

**Review request template**:
```
Subject: Thank you for choosing Pattern Growth!

Hi [Client Name],

I hope you're seeing great results from our growth strategy sprint!

If you're happy with the strategic architecture and team training we delivered, would you mind leaving us a quick review on Google? It really helps other growth-stage companies find the right marketing consulting partner.

Here's the link: [Google Review Link]

Thank you for your trust and partnership!

Best regards,
Ryan
Founder, Pattern Growth
```

#### **3. Respond to All Reviews**
- **Positive reviews**: Thank them and mention something specific
- **Negative reviews**: Apologize and offer to make it right
- **Response template**: "Thank you for your feedback. We're glad the [specific outcome] helped your team achieve [specific goal]."

---

### **Phase 4: Ongoing Management (Monthly)**

#### **1. Update Information**
- Review and update hours if they change
- Update services as you add new offerings
- Add new photos regularly
- Update description if your positioning changes

#### **2. Monitor Performance**
- Check Google Business Profile insights monthly
- Track calls, direction requests, and website clicks
- Identify which search terms people use to find you

#### **3. Post Updates**
**Content ideas**:
- New blog post announcements
- Case study summaries (without client names)
- Service updates
- Team additions
- Industry insights

**Posting schedule**: 2-3 posts per month
