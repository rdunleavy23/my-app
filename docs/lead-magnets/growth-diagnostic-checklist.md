## What Your Score Means

### 65-85 Points: **You Need a Strategy Sprint**

**Why:**
- You have execution capability (strong team)
- Data exists but isn't unified
- Strategic clarity is the bottleneck
- You'll own and run the strategy independently

**What you need:**
- Strategic architecture (positioning, ICP, frameworks)
- Unified measurement system (dashboard, attribution)
- Executable playbooks you can run
- Complete ownership transfer

**Best fit**: 8-week strategy sprint with full deliverables

**Not a fit**: Ongoing fractional CMO (you don't need operational oversight)

---

### 40-64 Points: **You Might Need Both (Start with Sprint)**

**Why:**
- Strategic gaps exist
- Some execution capability
- Data and systems need work

**What you need:**
- Strategic foundation first (sprint)
- Then evaluate if you need operational support (fractional CMO)
- Or build internal capability with strategy in hand

**Best fit**: Start with strategy sprint, reassess after

**Consider**: Fractional CMO only after strategic architecture exists

---

### Below 40 Points: **You Probably Need Fractional CMO or Full-Time Help**

**Why:**
- Major gaps in multiple areas
- Team needs hands-on leadership
- Execution capability is weak

**What you need:**
- Day-to-day operational management
- Team building and vendor oversight
- Ongoing strategic guidance

**Best fit**: Fractional CMO for 6-12 months

**Or**: Full-time marketing leader if you can afford it
