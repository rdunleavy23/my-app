# Keyword Research 2025 — Pattern Growth

_Last updated: 2025-10-27 (initial draft)_

## Methodology
- Data sources: Ahrefs (US), Google Search Console (last 3 months), manual SERP inspections (Oct 2025).
- Metrics defined:
  - **SV**: Monthly search volume (Ahrefs).
  - **KD**: Ahrefs keyword difficulty score.
  - **Intent**: Informational (I), Commercial Investigation (C), Transactional (T), Navigational (N).
  - **SERP Notes**: Featured snippets (FS), People Also Ask (PAA), Video, Local pack, Top ranking competitors.
- Keywords grouped by pillar/cluster alignment.
