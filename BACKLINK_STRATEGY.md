## ❌ WHAT NOT TO DO

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Buying backlinks (Fiverr, SEO services)
- ❌ Link farms or PBNs
- ❌ Spam comments with links
- ❌ Mass directory submissions (low-quality)
- ❌ Automated link building
- ❌ Reciprocal link schemes
- ❌ Paid links without `rel="nofollow"`

**Why:** Google penalizes these practices. Focus on earning legitimate links through great content and relationships.
